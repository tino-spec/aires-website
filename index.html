Our story

From Cape Town
to Burgau.

The head of our family, Aires Tolentino Ferreira, emigrated to beautiful Cape Town in the late 1940s. We named our restaurant in his honour. Some of you know us as “Burgau Meu Irmão”.

We are Aires Justino, a Portuguese national born and raised in Cape Town, and Darlene, born in Zimbabwe. In 2021 we came back to Portugal for a better, safer life and to be closer to our children in the UK. After three months in Aveiro, a road trip to the Algarve brought us to Burgau by pure chance. We had stumbled on our new home, and in August 2021 Bar Restaurante Aires was born.

Our family has a lifelong history in the fish industry, but here we cook our favourite braai food on the grill. Our promise: the finest quality food, with the warmest South African love.
